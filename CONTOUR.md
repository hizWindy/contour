## Company background

**Contour Commercial Interiors** (trading as *Contour Interiors*) is an Australian commercial workplace consultancy. It designs and delivers office fitouts, with strategy as the thread running through every project.

- **Positioning:** "Workplaces are strategic tools. We make them work harder." Contour works with leadership teams at the point where strategy, risk and investment decisions meet, and treats the workplace as a tool for delivering business priorities.
- **Typical triggers:** lease expiry, growth planning, rethinking how people work, relocation.
- **Integrated team:** designers, strategists and architects working alongside engineers, builders and project managers, so strategy, design and delivery run in parallel instead of in sequence.
- **Footprint:** case studies across Sydney, Melbourne, Perth and Adelaide.
- **Clients and projects (selection):** Bunge HQ, QUBE (Sydney and Melbourne), TPG Telecom (Perth and Melbourne), IG Australia, UCB Australia, Moore Australia, NHVR Adelaide, Sumitomo Perth, McConnell Dowell, 4D Medical, Softel Systems.
- **Contact:** 1300 200 750 · info@contourinteriors.com.au

### How Contour works: six stages

| Stage | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Research & Insight | Sector research, thematic deep dives, thought pieces, white papers |
| Strategic Advisory | Qualitative and quantitative analysis, benchmarking, occupancy modelling, briefing, change management |
| Workplace Design | Space planning and test fits, look and feel, materials and FF&E, documentation, consultant coordination |
| Pre-Construction | Programme, cost planning, value management, procurement and tender management |
| Project Delivery | Site management, trade coordination, quality assurance, defect management, reporting |
| Post Occupancy | Post-occupancy evaluation, utilisation review, staff feedback, ongoing optimisation |

### Content and thought leadership

The Insights section publishes articles on office design ROI, commercial lease negotiation, change management in relocations, lease termination and workplace strategy for office hunts.

### Where AI could add value (starting hypotheses, not commitments)

The six stages above are the natural places to look for opportunities:

- **Research & Insight:** drafting and summarising sector research, and keeping the Insights articles and case studies consistent.
- **Strategic Advisory:** analysing survey, utilisation and occupancy data, benchmarking, and turning stakeholder engagement into structured findings.
- **Workplace Design:** supporting briefs, test-fit options and materials or FF&E selection.
- **Pre-Construction and Delivery:** cost and programme tracking, tender comparison, reporting, and spotting risks and defects early.
- **Post Occupancy:** processing feedback and utilisation data to measure results against the original objectives.
- **Business operations:** proposals, bids, case-study writing and internal knowledge search across past projects.

## What they say together

- **Show the product** as the hero image, not a stock photo (7, 10, 12).
- **Say one thing:** one headline, one message, room around it (2, 10).
- **Put proof beside the claim,** and set numbers large (1, 3, 5, 11).
- **A bento of product pieces,** each tile showing one part of the product (12).
- **Only show proof that is real.** Ratings, avatar stacks, "10K+ users", logos and quotes (3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 12, 13) need to be backed. Decoration like a giant word behind the subject or hotspots (1, 2, 5, 8) explains nothing.

## Takeaway

The references agree on structure more than on style: show the product, say one thing, put the proof next to the claim, and let numbers be large. Leave the looks behind: purple, cut-out people, glass navigation pills, fake ratings.

# Research parameters

How the research agent turns the company criteria ([../criteria/COMPANY.md](../criteria/COMPANY.md)) into searches and facts.

Last updated 2026-10-23. **TBC** marks ideas not tested yet.

## What it records for each company

| Fact | Example |
|---|---|
| Name and website | ABC Logistics |
| City, and staff in that city | Perth, 300 |
| Estimated office size and project value | 3,000 m², $4.5M |
| Industry | Logistics |
| Owner | Private, Australian |
| Trigger and its date | Won a $200M contract, 14 Oct 2026 |
| Expected move date | 2027 (estimate) |
| Already in HubSpot? | No |

Every fact has a **source link**, a **date**, and a label: **confirmed**, **estimated** or **guessed**. This lets Levi check a lead in seconds.

## Where it looks for each trigger

| Trigger | Where |
|---|---|
| Lease ending | Property Daily, annual reports, property news |
| Merger or takeover | ASX announcements, news |
| Big contract or fast growth | ASX announcements, news, job ads |
| Return to office or restructure | News |
| New CEO or hiring push | News, careers pages (weak signs) |

## Weekly settings

| Setting | Version 1 |
|---|---|
| Cities | Melbourne, Adelaide, Perth |
| Triggers | Lease, merger, contract, growth |
| How far back | 30 days the first time, then 7 days each week |
| Watchlist | HubSpot target lists, checked by name |
| Limits | Most companies per week, and a spending cap (**TBC**) |

## Ideas to test

1. **Count staff in the city.** A bank with 2,000 staff may have only 40 in Adelaide. The local office is the one that moves.
2. **Best timing is 1 to 3 years before the move.** Any closer, and a consultant is probably already hired. (**TBC**)
3. **Use the WGEA list.** Private employers with 100+ staff report to the Workplace Gender Equality Agency, which publishes them by industry and size. It could be the starting list of companies. (**TBC**: does it show the city, and can we use it?)
4. **Watch building news.** When a building is sold or redeveloped, every tenant must move. One story can give several leads.
5. **Watch the fitout cycle.** Companies refit every 5 to 10 years. A client from 6 or more years ago may be due again.

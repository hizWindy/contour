# Company criteria, version 1

**Preliminary.** Levi to confirm. Last updated 2026-10-25. This is the version the engine follows today. The next draft is [Criteria_v2.md](Criteria_v2.md).

These rules decide which companies the research agent looks for, **before anyone calls them**. They come from three places: what Levi said on 20 and 23 Oct 2026, Contour's 11 deal rules ([DEAL.md](DEAL.md)), and Contour's own deals in HubSpot ([EXPORT.md](../research/EXPORT.md)). Rules marked **TBC** are our proposals.

A company needs two things: it **fits** (section 1), and it **needs an office change** (section 2). Score boosters (section 4) then decide how high it ranks. How each rule is measured is in [PARAMETERS.md](../research/PARAMETERS.md).

An office change is a move **or** a full refit. Example: ABC Logistics renews its Perth lease but refits two floors. That is still a project.

## 1. Does it fit?

Fail any rule → **Drop**.

| # | Rule | Passes if | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Size | An office of 1,000 to 6,000 m² in that city. If the size isn't known: 100 to 600 staff there. That's a $1.5M to $10M fitout. | Levi, 20 and 23 Oct. Office size first: HubSpot deals (**TBC**). "In that city": question 6. 10 m² per person: question 8. |
| 2 | City | An office in Melbourne, Adelaide or Perth | Levi, deal criteria |
| 3 | Industry | The office matters to how they work: teamwork, clients, hiring. Site-heavy firms, like builders and miners, are not dropped for their industry. They get a "check office size" flag. | Deal criteria. The flag: HubSpot deals (**TBC**) |
| 4 | Local team | The local team can run the project, even if the company is owned overseas | **TBC** |
| 5 | Healthy | Not in administration, and not cutting staff fast | **TBC** |

Rule 4 was "decides locally." It changed because at least 4 of the 11 clients on Contour's website are Australian arms of overseas companies: Bunge, IG, UCB and Sumitomo. The old rule could drop leads like them. Their ownership is from general knowledge (**TBC**).

## 2. Does it need an office change?

It needs at least one trigger: a sign of an office change within 3 years. No trigger yet → **Watch**.

| Group | Triggers | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Main | Lease ending in 1 to 3 years. Merger or takeover, where one company has an old office. Big new contract, needing about 100 more office staff. Fast growth. | Levi, 23 Oct. Ranking: question 3. Numbers: question 4. |
| Other | Merging several offices into one. Return to office. Restructure. | Deal criteria |
| Weak | New CEO or culture change. Hiring push to attract talent. | Deal criteria |

## 3. Sent to a Director

These fit and have a trigger, but a Director decides:
- Over about 600 staff or 6,000 m². This may be a Whale Pipeline project.
- An office of 800 to 1,000 m², just under the minimum. Contour validated a deal at 800 m² (Alvarez & Marsal). **TBC**: this relaxes Levi's 1,000 m² rule.
- Sydney or another city.
- Government, education, industry consultants, law firms (especially the biggest), premium banks and finance, real estate agencies, standard not-for-profits, hospitals and healthcare. Excluded or case by case: question 7. The HubSpot deals point to case by case: Shakespeare Financial and Alvarez & Marsal are live deals.

## 4. Score boosters

All **TBC**. The more boosters a company has, the higher it ranks. They add to the score in [LEADS.md](../LEADS.md): trigger type, how recent, fit result and source.

| Booster | What to look for | Deal rule |
|---|---|---|
| Property Daily move | A relocation or lease ending found in Property Daily. It scores highest of all signs. | Need, Timing |
| Office is due | Leases rather than owns. 6+ years in the same office, since fitouts come every 5 to 10 years. | Need |
| Buys strategy | Talks about culture, hybrid work or green-building ratings (like NABERS or Green Star). Hires firms directly, not through price-only tenders. | Early engagement |
| Can pay | Profitable, growing or recently raised money | Likelihood |
| No rival in place | Not in a competitor's case studies from the last 5 years | Competition |
| Reachable decision maker | A known role (COO, CFO, Head of Property) or a contact already in HubSpot | Decision makers |

## 5. Never suggest

- Companies already sent
- Companies with an open deal or an active project in HubSpot
- Companies whose deal was dissolved in the last 12 months, unless a new trigger appears. The 12 months is a guess (**TBC**).
- Companies on a do-not-contact list

**Not used:** how often Contour has contacted a company. Levi said on 23 Oct that it doesn't predict projects.

## 6. Result

| Result | When |

| **Director review** | Fits and has a trigger, but is on the Director list (section 3) |

## 7. What Contour's HubSpot deals show

From the 12 deals in the HubSpot export of 25 Oct 2026. Details in [EXPORT.md](../research/EXPORT.md). Only 12 deals, so all of this is **TBC**.

- **Size is measured in m².** Every deal is named by its m², and every amount is m² × $1,500. Across 8 deals, m² per person runs from 2.7 to 18 (median about 8), so staff × 10 m² is only a rough guess. → Rule 1.
- **Just under 1,000 m² can still win.** 4 of 12 deals are under 1,000 m². Alvarez & Marsal at 800 m² is "Contour Validated." → Section 3.
- **Industry alone is a weak test.** The 22 construction, civil and mining companies have no deals. But Nilsen Group, an electrical firm, has a 1,000 m² office deal. → Rule 3.
- **Property Daily leads hold up best.** 4 of 5 "PD Relocation" deals are still open. 3 of 4 "INTEL" deals were dissolved. → Section 4.
- **Deals often fall through.** 5 of 12 were dissolved, with no reason recorded (question 9). → Section 5.

## 8. How the 11 deal rules carry over

Every deal rule feeds in. The tool checks 5 directly from public information. The other 6 it can only judge from clues, and sales confirms them after the first call. The full rules are in [DEAL.md](DEAL.md).

| Deal rule | Used here as | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Size ($1.5M to $10M) | Fit rule 1, and the 800 to 1,000 m² Director band | Direct |
| Location | Fit rule 2 | Direct |
| Industry | Fit rule 3, and the Director list | Direct |
| Procurement | Fit rule 4 | Direct |
| Timing (within 3 years) | The trigger date, or a lease ending in 1 to 3 years | Direct |
| Need | A trigger (section 2), "Property Daily move" and "Office is due" | Clues |
| Likelihood (over 50%) | "Can pay", a strong source, or more than one trigger | Clues |
| Competition | "No rival in place" | Clues |
| Other red flags | Fit rule 5, and the never-suggest list | Clues |
| Decision makers | "Reachable decision maker" | Clues |
| Early engagement | "Buys strategy" | Clues |

The numbers line up. 100 staff × 10 m² = 1,000 m², and at $1,500 per m² that's $1.5M, the bottom of the deal range. 600 staff × 10 m² = 6,000 m², the top. So the 600-staff Director limit matches the deal criteria. Contour's HubSpot deals use the same $1,500 per m².

# Company criteria

**Draft.** Levi to confirm. Last updated 2026-10-24.

These rules decide which companies the research agent looks for, **before anyone calls them**. They come from what Levi said on 20 and 23 Oct 2026, and from the deal criteria ([DEAL.md](DEAL.md)). Rules marked **TBC** are our proposals.

A company needs two things: it **fits**, and it has a **trigger**.

## 1. Does it fit?

| Rule | Passes if | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Staff | 100+ staff in that city | Levi, 20 and 23 Oct |
| Office size | 1,000 m² or more. Estimated as staff × 10 m². | Levi, 23 Oct |
| City | Melbourne, Adelaide or Perth | Levi, deal criteria |
| Industry | The office matters to how they work: teamwork, clients, hiring | Deal criteria |
| Office staff | Most staff work in an office, not on sites or in shops | **TBC** |
| Decides locally | Decisions are made in Australia, not overseas | **TBC** |
| Healthy | Not in administration or shrinking fast | **TBC** |

**Sent to a Director instead of passing:**
- Over about 600 staff (a very large project)
- Sydney or another city
- Government, education, industry consultants, law firms (especially the biggest), premium banks and finance, real estate agencies, standard not-for-profits, hospitals and healthcare

## 2. Does it have a trigger?

A sign it may need a new office within 3 years.

**Main triggers** (Levi, 23 Oct):
- Lease ending
- Merger or takeover, where one company has an old office
- Big new contract, needing about 100 more office staff
- Fast growth

**Other triggers** (deal criteria):
- Merging several offices into one
- Return to office
- Restructure
- New CEO or culture change (weak sign)
- Hiring push to attract talent (weak sign)

## 3. Result

| Result | When |
|---|---|
| **Pass** | Fits and has a trigger |
| **Director review** | Fits and has a trigger, but is on the Director list above |
| **Watch** | Fits, but no trigger yet. Saved and re-checked every week. |
| **Drop** | Under 100 staff, or fails another fit rule |

## Never suggest

- Companies already sent
- Companies with an open deal or an active project in HubSpot
- Companies on a do-not-contact list

**Not used:** how often Contour has contacted a company. Levi said on 23 Oct that it doesn't predict projects.

## 4. How the 11 deal rules carry over

Five deal rules can be checked from public information. Four can only be judged from clues, and sales confirms them after the first call. Two need a conversation, so only sales can check them. The full rules are in [DEAL.md](DEAL.md).

| Deal rule | What the tool checks | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Size ($1.5M to $10M) | 100 to 600 staff in that city | Direct |
| Location | An office in Melbourne, Adelaide or Perth | Direct |
| Industry | Industry type. Director-review industries are flagged. | Direct |
| Procurement | Australian head office, or decisions made locally | Direct |
| Timing (within 3 years) | Date of the trigger, or a lease ending in 1 to 3 years | Direct |
| Need | A trigger from section 2 | Clues |
| Likelihood (over 50%) | A strong source, or more than one trigger | Clues |
| Competition | No news of a fitout firm already appointed | Clues |
| Other red flags | A healthy company with no open deal in HubSpot | Clues |
| Decision makers | Suggests a role to contact, like CFO | Sales |
| Early engagement | Can't be seen from outside | Sales |

The numbers line up. 100 staff × 10 m² = 1,000 m², and at $1,500 per m² that's $1.5M, the bottom of the deal range. 600 staff × 10 m² = 6,000 m², the top. So the 600-staff Director limit matches the deal criteria. Over 600 may be a Whale Pipeline project.

## 5. What the tool works out for each company

| Estimate | How |
|---|---|
| Office size | Staff × 10 m² |
| Fitout value | Office size × $1,500 per m², the minimum rate in the deal criteria. 300 staff → 3,000 m² → at least $4.5M. |
| Move window | From the trigger date or the lease end date. The best window is 1 to 3 years out (**TBC**). |
| Who to contact | A role, like CFO or Head of Property. Names come later. |
| How sure it is | Each fact is marked confirmed, estimated or guessed, with a source link |

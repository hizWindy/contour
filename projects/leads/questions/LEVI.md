# Questions for Levi

On 23 Oct 2026, Levi asked for the questions and information needed to build the research tool. He asked in the meeting, and again by email.

## Summary of our discussion

- Every week, the tool finds companies that **fit** Contour and show **signs of moving**.
- **Fits:** 100+ staff and 1,000 m²+ in Melbourne, Adelaide or Perth, in an industry where the office matters.
- **Signs of moving:** lease ending, merger, big new contract, fast growth. A move is likely within 3 years.
- Claude searches the web and Contour's HubSpot target lists. Levi approves each lead, then HubSpot hands it to a salesperson.

## Claude's view

Searching the web is the easy part. What Claude can't know is what Contour knows. The three most useful things Contour can provide:

1. **Past wins.** What was happening at each company before it called Contour. This teaches the tool what to look for, and lets us test it.
2. **Property Daily access.** Lease endings are the strongest sign, and they're rarely public.
3. **Numbers for the triggers.** For example, how many new jobs counts as "fast growth"? Without numbers, the tool guesses.

## The questions

**Already answered by the deal criteria, so not asked here:** project size, cities, industries and timing.

**If there's only time for five:** questions 1, 5, 7, 8 and 11.

### 1. Confirm the company criteria

See [../criteria/COMPANY.md](../criteria/COMPANY.md).

1. Is there an official company criteria document? You mentioned one on 20 Oct.
2. Are the Director-review industries excluded, or reviewed case by case?
3. Does 100+ staff mean in that city, or across the whole company?
4. Is 10 m² per person right for estimating office size?

### 2. Real examples (most important)

5. What are your 10 to 20 best past projects? For each one: company, city, staff, and what was happening when they first spoke to Contour.
6. Which deals were lost or a bad fit, and why?

### 3. Triggers

7. Rank the main triggers: lease ending, merger, big contract, fast growth. Which ones led to real projects?
8. What numbers count? For example, how many new jobs is "fast growth"? How big is a "big contract"?
9. How old can a trigger be and still matter?
10. Which competitors should we watch?

### 4. Data Contour already has

11. What does the Property Daily access include? Lease end dates?
12. Which HubSpot lists should the tool check each week?
13. What other paid tools does Contour use? For example LinkedIn Sales Navigator or ZoomInfo.
14. What does the team know that isn't written down? A simple spreadsheet is enough.

### 5. Running it

15. How many leads a week? Our suggestion is 10 to 20.
16. Who gets which lead: by city, by industry, or taking turns?
17. Contacts: name and role only, or email and phone too? What does compliance say?
18. What is the monthly budget for AI and data tools?
19. Is scraping allowed? Who signs it off?
20. What would success look like after 3 months?

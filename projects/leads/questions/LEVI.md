## Summary of our discussion

- Every week, the tool finds companies that **fit** Contour and show **signs of moving**.
- **Fits:** 100 to 600 staff and 1,000 m²+ in Melbourne, Adelaide or Perth, in an industry where the office matters.
- **Signs of moving:** lease ending, merger, big new contract, fast growth. A move is likely within 3 years.
- Claude searches the web and Contour's HubSpot target lists. Levi approves each lead, then HubSpot hands it to a salesperson.

## Claude's view

Searching the web is the easy part. What Claude can't know is what Contour knows. The three most useful things Contour can provide:

1. **Past wins and losses.** What was happening at each company before it called Contour. This teaches the tool what to look for, and lets us test it.
2. **Numbers for the triggers.** For example, how many new jobs counts as "fast growth"? Without numbers, the tool guesses.
3. **Lease data.** Lease endings are the strongest sign, and they're rarely public. Property Daily has them. Levi sent the logins (by 24 Oct). Still to check: what the data includes, and whether its terms let a program read it.
